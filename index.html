About Me

Geospatial and Earth Observation professional with 6+ years of experience in GIS, remote sensing, spatial modelling, environmental monitoring, conservation planning, and geospatial data management. I work with satellite and aerial imagery, large spatial datasets, machine-learning workflows, and spatial decision-support methods to translate complex geospatial information into reliable operational and research outputs.

My technical toolkit includes Python, GeoPandas, Rasterio, GDAL, Google Earth Engine, QGIS, ArcGIS Pro, PostgreSQL/PostGIS, SQL, Leaflet, and geospatial web services. My work spans Earth Observation, automated image analysis, environmental and biodiversity modelling, suitability analysis, conservation prioritisation, WebGIS, and reproducible geospatial workflows.

Selected Projects

A selection of geospatial, remote sensing, conservation planning, and spatial decision-support projects. Click each project to view the full case study.

Green Roof Detection in Baden-Württemberg

Developed a state-scale Earth Observation and machine-learning workflow combining 14+ million LoD2 roof planes with multispectral PlanetScope imagery, Python, and Random Forest classification to produce a probability-based green-roof inventory for Baden-Württemberg.

Python · GeoPandas · PlanetScope · LoD2 · Remote Sensing · Random Forest · Large-Scale Geoprocessing

Resilient MPA Network for the Arafura and Timor Seas

Applied GIS-based spatial prioritisation and systematic conservation planning across Indonesia, Timor-Leste, Australia and Papua New Guinea, integrating ecological features, human-use constraints, cost surfaces and Marxan optimisation to design a resilient transboundary MPA network.

Spatial Prioritisation · Marxan · Optimization · GIS · Conservation Planning · Multi-Criteria Analysis

Mangrove and Aquaculture Dynamics in East Kalimantan

Mapped nearly three decades of mangrove loss and aquaculture expansion in East Kalimantan, combining satellite analysis with field-based LAI, biomass, and carbon-stock estimation.

Google Earth Engine - Landsat - Sentinel-2 - LAI - Carbon Stock

Marine Protected Area Network Design for FMA715

Applied systematic conservation planning and spatial optimization using Marxan across FMA715 and six Indonesian provinces, integrating biodiversity targets, fisheries, infrastructure, human-use constraints and stakeholder-defined planning priorities.

Spatial Prioritisation · Marxan · Optimization · GIS · Conservation Planning · Multi-Criteria Analysis

Shallow-Water Habitat Mapping

Combined field sampling, underwater photography, CPCE interpretation, and Lyzenga water-column correction to classify shallow-water benthic habitats.

Marine Remote Sensing · CPCE · Lyzenga · Field Survey · GIS

Stuttgart Urban Vegetation Mapping

Compared U-Net semantic segmentation and YOLOv11 object detection for mapping urban vegetation from 8 cm aerial imagery of Stuttgart.

PyTorch · U-Net · YOLOv11 · RGB/NIR · LabelStudio

Multi-Criteria GIS Site Suitability Analysis

Applied fuzzy GIS suitability modelling to environmental and accessibility criteria, comparing weighted scoring with multiplicative overlay to identify robust landfill candidate areas.

Site Screening · Multi-Criteria Analysis · Raster GIS · Suitability Modelling · QGIS
