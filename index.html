About Me

I am a geospatial professional with 6+ years of experience in GIS, remote sensing, spatial modelling, and geodata management for environmental, conservation, and public-sector projects. My work focuses on transforming complex spatial datasets into decision-support products, including thematic maps, spatial models, WebGIS applications, and reproducible geodata workflows.

I have worked with satellite imagery, aerial imagery, field data, participatory mapping outputs, administrative datasets, and official geodata. My technical background includes QGIS, ArcGIS Pro, Python, GeoPandas, PostGIS, Google Earth Engine, Leaflet, Flask, GeoServer, and machine learning for geospatial applications.

Selected Projects

Marine Protected Area Network Design

Supported the design and optimization of Marine Protected Area networks using MARXAN, gap analysis, and spatial prioritization. The work focused on improving protection for shallow-water habitats, deep-water habitats, and key conservation features using principles of connectivity, adequacy, representativeness, and efficiency.

Tools: MARXAN, QGIS, ArcGIS, spatial prioritization, conservation planning

Green Roof Detection in Baden-Württemberg

Developed geospatial workflows for identifying green roof areas using LoD2 building geometry and PlanetScope imagery. The project involved roof-plane data preparation, feature extraction, quality control, and machine-learning-ready datasets for a 2025 green roof inventory.

Tools: Python, GeoPandas, PlanetScope, LoD2 CityGML, machine learning

Stuttgart Landfill Suitability Analysis

Conducted GIS-based site suitability analysis for landfill location planning. The workflow considered spatial constraints, exclusion zones, accessibility, environmental sensitivity, and planning-relevant criteria to identify suitable candidate areas.

Tools: QGIS, ArcGIS Pro, multi-criteria analysis, spatial overlay

IKEA Franchise Location Expansion Analysis

Applied GIS-based business location analysis to support potential franchise expansion. The analysis considered accessibility, population distribution, market reach, transport connectivity, and spatial competition factors.

Tools: GIS, accessibility analysis, market area analysis, spatial decision support

Urban Vegetation Mapping

Contributed to urban vegetation mapping using high-resolution aerial imagery and deep learning methods. The project focused on extracting vegetation information from complex urban environments to support urban ecological assessment and green infrastructure analysis.

Tools: Aerial imagery, deep learning, image classification, GIS

Multitemporal Mangrove and Aquaculture Mapping

Developed remote-sensing workflows for mapping mangrove and aquaculture dynamics over time. The work used multi-temporal satellite imagery to monitor coastal landscape change and support environmental management in Indonesia.

Tools: Google Earth Engine, Landsat, Sentinel, Planet NICFI, change detection

Shallow-Water Habitat Mapping

Applied remote sensing methods to classify shallow-water habitats, including coral, seagrass, sand, and rubble. The project supported coastal and marine conservation planning through spatial habitat information for Indonesian coastal waters.

Tools: Satellite imagery, remote sensing, habitat classification, coastal GIS
