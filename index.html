About Me

I am a geospatial professional with 6+ years of experience in GIS, remote sensing, spatial modelling, and geodata management for environmental, conservation, and public-sector projects. My work focuses on transforming complex spatial datasets into decision-support products, including thematic maps, spatial models, WebGIS applications, and reproducible geodata workflows.

I have worked with satellite imagery, aerial imagery, field data, participatory mapping outputs, administrative datasets, and official geodata. My technical background includes QGIS, ArcGIS Pro, Python, GeoPandas, PostGIS, Google Earth Engine, Leaflet, Flask, GeoServer, and machine learning for geospatial applications.
